What is DevOps Lounge?

A free Discord community for developers and DevOps engineers at every level. Members ask questions, share projects, review code and find people to build with.

Is it free?

Yes. Joining is free and there is nothing to buy.

I am just starting out. Is it for me?

Yes. There is a beginner track and a beginner corner, and every help forum welcomes first questions. Experienced engineers are there too, which is what makes the answers good.

What languages can I talk in?

English is shared by everyone, and there are rooms in Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Russian.

What topics does it cover?

Ten tracks: AI, web, app development, frontend, backend, mobile, data and ML, security, DevOps and cloud, and game development, plus career and interview help.

How do I get help with my code?

Post in the ask-a-question forum with what you tried and the error you see. When it is solved, the thread is tagged so others can find the answer later. There is also a code review forum.

Pull up a chair.

Join, pick your language and what you work on, and say hi. It takes a minute.
