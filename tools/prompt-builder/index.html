Prompt Builder

Write your idea the way you would say it. The builder spots your language, framework, error and visual style, and turns it into a prompt that gets better answers.

    How it works
    • It reads your wordsA dictionary of 15 languages and 34 frameworks and services picks out your stack. Error lines are found by their shape.
    • It adds what experts addA role, the context, clear requirements and the exact output you want. For images: style, light, lens and the right syntax for your model.
    • It grades the promptThe checklist shows what is missing, like the error message or the language, so the answer is right the first time.
    
  
    Questions
    Does it use AI?
    No. It runs a set of rules in your browser, so it is instant, free and private. Use the prompt it builds with any AI assistant you like.
    Which image models does it support?
    Midjourney (with --ar and --no), Stable Diffusion (with a negative prompt), Flux and ChatGPT image generation.
    Is my idea sent anywhere?
    No. Everything happens on this page. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
