llms.txt generator

Paste your sitemap.xml or a list of pages. Get a clean llms.txt that tells AI assistants what your site is and which pages matter, ready to upload to your site's root.


      

    

  



  
  
How it works
  • The llms.txt formatA Markdown file at your site's root: a title, a one-line summary in a quote, optional context, then sections of links. Less important pages go under Optional.
  • Grouped for youPages are grouped by the first part of their path, the largest groups first, and legal or archive pages move to Optional.
  • Make it yoursRename the link titles and add a short note after each link so assistants know when to use it.

  
Questions
Where do I put it?
At the root of your site, so it opens at yoursite.com/llms.txt, next to robots.txt.
Does it replace a sitemap?
No. The sitemap is for search engines; llms.txt is a short, readable guide for AI assistants. Keep both.
