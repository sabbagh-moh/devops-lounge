    How it works

    • JavaScript flavorPatterns run with the JavaScript engine in your browser, the same one Node.js uses.
    • Flagsg finds every match, i ignores case, m makes ^ and $ work per line, s lets . match new lines, u enables full Unicode.
    • GroupsParentheses capture parts of a match. They are listed as $1, $2 and so on.

    Questions

    Why does my pattern work here but not in Python?

    Regex flavors differ a little. Lookbehind, named groups and some escapes are written differently in Python, PCRE and JavaScript.

    Is my text sent anywhere?

    No. Matching happens in your browser.
