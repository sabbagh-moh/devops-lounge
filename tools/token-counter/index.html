    Rules of thumb

    TextRoughly
    1 token4 characters of English, or about 3/4 of a word
    100 tokensAbout 75 English words
    1,000 words of EnglishAbout 1,300 tokens
    A page of codeAbout 500 to 800 tokens
    Arabic or Russian textOften 1.5 to 3 times the tokens of the same length in English

    How it works

    • An honest estimateCommon English words are one token, long words several, and Arabic, Russian and Chinese text use more tokens per character. The range covers how real tokenizers differ.
    • Context windowsThe bars show how much of each common window size your text fills, before the model writes a word back.
    • CostEnter your model's current input price to see what one request costs. Prices change, so take them from your provider.

    Questions

    Is this exact?

    It is an estimate, usually within 10 to 15 percent. Exact counts need the model's own tokenizer, and every model family has a different one.

    Why does Arabic use more tokens?

    Tokenizers are trained mostly on English, so other scripts get split into smaller pieces.

    Is my text sent anywhere?

    No. Counting happens in your browser.
